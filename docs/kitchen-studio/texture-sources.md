# Image textures / Poly Haven
The six photographic base-colour maps below were downloaded from Poly Haven's public texture downloads and resized/re-encoded as local 768px WebP for the app. They supply image variation for a design sample; changing a sample's assigned colour or finish does not turn it into a certified supplier material. All assets are CC0: https://polyhaven.com/license
- Oak veneer: https://polyhaven.com/a/oak_veneer_01
- Stained furniture wood: https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_table_001
- Marble: https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_01
- Granite: https://polyhaven.com/a/granite_tile
- Wood flooring: https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_floor
- Stone flooring: https://polyhaven.com/a/stone_tiles

Local files use the asset IDs as their filenames under dist/studio-textures/. Photos assigned to a wall by the designer stay on their device and are embedded when a portable JSON project is exported.

Window environment: Garden Nook, photographed by Dimitrios Savva and processed by Jarod Guest, CC0. The public 1K Radiance HDR was tone mapped for browser display as garden_nook.webp. https://polyhaven.com/a/garden_nook
